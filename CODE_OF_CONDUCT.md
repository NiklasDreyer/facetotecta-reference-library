# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is maintained by researchers in several countries and welcomes contributors of every background, career stage and level of experience. We are committed to making participation a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and science, not about people.
- Credit the work and data of others, including collectors, taxonomists and data providers.
- Respect the rights of the countries and communities that provided samples, in line with the Nagoya Protocol.
- Help newcomers find their way around the code.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Using data or results from this project in ways that misrepresent their source or authorship.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers: Niklas Dreyer (dreyer@snm.ku.dk) or Benny K. K. Chan (chankk@as.sinica.edu.tw). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation in the project.

## Attribution

This code of conduct follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
